Privacy Policy for Little Later

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Little Later ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to help you save links, organize ideas into personal and collaborative folders, and set helpful reminders to revisit them later. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or your email, we collect your name and email address to authenticate your account and securely associate your cloud backup and collaborative folders.
  • User Content: The links, notes, checklists, reminders, and folder names you create inside the app are stored locally on your device and, if signed in, synchronized to our secure Google Firebase database for cross-device access and collaboration.
  • Device & Notification Data: We utilize local notification alarms on Android to deliver scheduled reminders you explicitly set. We do not track your location or access personal files.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, sync, and maintain your saved links, folders, and reminders across your devices.
  • To enable collaborative folders between you and friends you explicitly invite via invite links.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or saved links to third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your cloud data is securely hosted using Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure adhering to enterprise security standards. Personal data is only accessible to your authenticated account and collaborators you invite.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You can remove any saved link, note, or folder directly inside the app at any time. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data, you can do so in the app settings or by contacting us.

5. Contact Us
